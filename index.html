About All Trains In A Day

A 24-hour interactive visualization of Swedish passenger rail traffic across the national railway network.

  • 3,300+ daily trains simulated along real OpenStreetMap tracks and 7-day Swedish GTFS timetables.
  • 24h Timeline: Scrub the bottom timeline to explore rush hours, daytime intercity traffic, and overnight sleeper trains.
  • Real-time Tracking: Click any moving train to track its speed, operator, and full journey.
  • Station Timetables: Zoom into cities (zoom ≥ 3.2× on mobile) to inspect live departures and arrivals boards.
